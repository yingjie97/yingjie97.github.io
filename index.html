Yingjie Cheng

Postdoctoral researcher in Astronomy

University of Washington (Seattle)


yingjiec@uw.edu

I am currently a postdoc in Astronomy at the University of Washington. I got my B.S. in Astronomy at Nanjing University in 2019, and PhD in Astronomy at UMass Amherst in 2025. My current research focuses on studying the formation and evolution of galaxies with JWST and ALMA data, in particular through the SED modeling of high-redshift galaxies.
